The Story of Swarsanskar

Swarsanskar Music Academy was founded by Shri Ranjit Gaonkar, a simple and humble man from the village of Ovliye in Sindhudurg. His uncle, Namdevbuva Ghadigaonkar, was a well-known Adhyatmik Bhajan artist of his time, while his father was an Adhyatmik scholar. Coming from such a rich spiritual and musical background, music naturally became an important part of his life.

Early Years · Katta

First Steps in Music

Ranjit Gaonkar began his music journey under Pt. Wamanrao Karane Guruji. Later, personal reasons brought him to Mumbai.

Mumbai · ~4–5 Years

Pakhawaj with Pt. Madhukar Mahadik

Trained in the Kudansingh Gharana Pakhawaj Solo style under the renowned maestro Pt. Madhukar Mahadik.

Continuing Study · 5–6 Years

Further Training under Pt. Prakash Sejwal

Polished his Pakhawaj skills further over several years of dedicated riyaaz.

Vocal Studies

Sangeet Visharad under Shri Rajarambuva Sutar

Completed Sangeet Visharad in Vocal music under the guidance of Shri Rajarambuva Sutar.

2007–08 · Kandivali

Academy Founded

Swarsanskar Music Academy first opened its doors in Kandivali, Mumbai.

2014 – Present · Vasai

Academy at Vasai

The academy has been running successfully from his residence in Vasai, guiding students in classical music, Pakhawaj, and Bhajan traditions.

Apart from being a dedicated musician and teacher, Shri Ranjit Gaonkar is also a Lawyer by profession — a remarkable combination of discipline, scholarship, and devotion.
