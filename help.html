Common Questions

Quick answers to what students usually ask

How to enroll?

Visit the academy or call the contact number. After a short guidance session, a suitable batch will be allotted.

Gandharva exam prep?

Yes — students are guided for ABGMV exams with proper syllabus planning and regular practice sessions.

Who can join?

Students from age 6 and above. Adult learners are equally welcome and placed in suitable batches.

Online classes?

Online and hybrid batches may be available depending on demand. Contact the academy for current availability.
